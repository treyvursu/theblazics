Our story

The Blazic Collective is Piper and Brittani.

The Blazic Collective started the way most family businesses do, with one person who kept making things and one person who said they should sell them.

Piper is the maker. She has run a bake sale since she was eight, started her wrapping business in 2025, and has been crafting for as long as she has been able to hold scissors. Now each one has a name, and what used to go to family and friends is open to everyone.

Brittani is the operator. She washes the dishes, keeps supplies ready to go, helps with the books, drives to every pickup and drop-off, and keeps the timeline so school and sports come first and the business still hits its deadlines on time.

We keep it small on purpose. Bake drops go out every Saturday, and preorders can be placed any day. Handmade pieces are made to order and can also be found at holiday markets. Gift wrap is done by hand, one package at a time.

Every order leaves with the same thing: a kraft tag stamped with the BC monogram and one line in Piper's handwriting. It means the same thing on a treat box, a craft piece or a wrapped gift: a person made this, and she wanted you to have it.
