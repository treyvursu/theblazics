Where it started

The bake sale stand turned into a business.

Blazic Bakes is where the collective started. Piper has run a bake sale since she was eight. She turns on the oven and bakes everything herself, after school and between practices.

Brittani makes sure she knows every order that came in, has the ingredients she needs, and has enough time blocked to get it done. Everything is boxed and tagged before it leaves.
