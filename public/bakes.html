Questions

Good to know.

How do I place an order?

Send a request through the contact form or email hello@theblazics.com with the line, the date you need it and what you would like. We reply with a confirmed date before anything is made.

When are bake drops?

Bake drops go out every Saturday. Preorders can be placed any day. The menu has a few weekly staples and one combo cookie that changes each week.

Are handmade pieces in stock?

Handmade pieces are made to order. Each season there is also a small table of finished work at holiday markets.

Do you wrap gifts I already bought?

Yes. Bring the gifts, boxed or not, and we wrap them by hand in paper and ribbon chosen for the person. Wrap season runs Thanksgiving to Christmas, so message early to check availability.

Where are you located?

We are based in Monticello, Indiana. Message us to talk through pickup for your order.

Who makes everything?

Piper bakes, crafts and wraps everything herself. Brittani handles supplies, the books, the calendar and every pickup and drop-off.

What comes with every order?

A kraft tag stamped with the BC monogram and one line in Piper's handwriting.
